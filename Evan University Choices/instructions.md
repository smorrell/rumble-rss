# Project Instructions

- Keep Evan's planning information organized in three files:
  - `inputs.md` for his background, courses, interests, and preferences used in the analysis.
  - `outputs.md` for program recommendations, analysis, admissions prerequisites, and entrance-mark information.
  - `questions.md` for outstanding questions that need answers.
- Recommend specific programs at specific Ontario universities, not generic program categories.
- Base recommendations on Evan's interests, strengths, preference to study away from home, work-life balance, and his questions: whether he will enjoy the program, whether it can lead to jobs, and whether he may enjoy the work.
- Check university-published admission requirements and prior-year entrance ranges where available. Clearly distinguish historical ranges, current guidance, and minimum prerequisites; do not invent missing information.
- Compare prerequisites with Evan's documented course codes and marks. State when eligibility cannot be confirmed and identify exactly what information is missing.
- Incorporate new notes, course codes, marks, and timetable information into the appropriate files, keeping `inputs.md` as the source of Evan-specific facts.
- Treat job and career examples as possibilities, not guarantees. Consider work settings and schedules against Evan's preferences.
