### Career-fit lens

The preferred predictable, mostly office-based schedule ending around 5 p.m. may be more common in some business, accounting, or education roles than in game development, product design, or creative production, but none guarantees remote work or fixed hours. Game, design, marketing, and media jobs can involve project deadlines; teaching follows a school calendar but includes preparation and work outside classroom hours. Compare actual graduate roles and talk to people doing the work before treating a program title as a lifestyle promise.
