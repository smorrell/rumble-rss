## Decision guidance

- **Best first visit/open-house picks:** Waterloo GBDA, Queen's Computing and the Creative Arts, Carleton IMD, and Laurier BBA. Compare what students actually do each week, not just program titles.
- **Best mix of creative interest and clearer business pathways:** Waterloo GBDA, Guelph Marketing Management, TMU Creative Industries, and Laurier BBA.
- **Best direct match for office-oriented work:** York Accounting or HRM, and business programs with co-op. Ask about workload peaks, hybrid-work availability, and entry-level job duties; a degree cannot guarantee remote work or a 5 p.m. finish.
- **Questions to test whether he'd enjoy the work:** Try a small video project with planned audience metrics; speak with an accountant, HR coordinator, marketer, and digital-media designer; and review sample first-year timetables/course descriptions.
- **Job prospects:** The programs list plausible career directions and some offer internships/co-op, but that is not the same as a placement guarantee. Compare current graduate employment outcomes, co-op participation/placement rates, and local job postings before deciding.

## Other pathways

Teaching generally requires an undergraduate degree followed by teacher education. Occupational therapy and speech-language pathology usually require further professional education; Evan's lack of senior science may matter for prerequisites, and those paths can include clinical placements. They are not prioritized in this undergraduate shortlist until requirements and interest are explored.
