# 2026 Max Partlo Memorial Tournament

## Tournament details

- **Dates:** December 4–6, 2026
- **Location:** Tillsonburg
- **Games:** Each team will play 4 round-robin games.
- **Official rules:** https://tmhi.org/Tournaments/3346/Pages/13174/Rules/
- **Schedule:** https://tmhi.org/Tournaments/3346/Schedule/

Check the rules and schedule pages regularly for updates before and during the tournament. Games may start up to 15 minutes early.

## Player and team requirements

- The tournament rules require **mouthguards and neck guards for all players**. Bring both for every game.
- The home team wears white jerseys. If the team has only one jersey set, the tournament director should be notified.
- A team may have up to 20 players and 5 bench staff. Everyone must be on the approved team roster.
- Coaches or team representatives must enter the roster in Gamesheet and add jersey numbers and AP players once the tournament invitation codes are sent.
- All bench staff must check in at the Tournament Office before each game to sign in on the iPads.

## At the arena

- The Tournament Office is on the lower level beside the Skate Shop and will be staffed during the weekend.
- Running and off-ice warmups are not permitted inside the community centre or arenas. Players may warm up only in their assigned dressing room or on their assigned ice.
- Each team selects one MVP per game; medals are available at the Tournament Office. Finalists and champions also receive medals. Photo banners are available but must be returned.
