This folder contains two related documents for the 2026 Durham West Girls Hockey Association (DWGHA) House League Tournament:

1. `2026 Durham West Houseleague Tournament Welcome Letter.docx.pdf`
2. `FW_ Welcome _Tournament Details.msg`

## Overview
The documents describe the 2026 annual DWGHA houseleague tournament, which will take place from November 27-29, 2026. The tournament is organized by Durham West Girls Hockey Association and is being run with support from the Tournkey platform. The materials are intended to welcome participating coaches and managers and provide logistics, schedule, venue, and contact information.

## Tournament details
- Event: 2026 Durham West Girls Hockey Association Annual House League Tournament
- Dates: November 27-29, 2026
- Location: Durham West / Ajax-area hockey venues
- Participating teams: 55 teams from multiple regions of Ontario
- Purpose: Welcome coaches and managers and provide tournament logistics

## Venue and logistics
- Tournament headquarters: Ajax Community Centre, upstairs outside the viewing area at Pad 1
- Coaches/managers should stop by headquarters Friday to pick up team loot bags and locker room keys
- Locker room keys must be left with headquarters staff and must be returned to ensure accountability
- Arena locations mentioned include:
  - Ajax Community Centre, 75 Centennial Rd
  - Chestnut Hill Complex (for U9 only), 1867 Valley Farm Rd, with the arena at the back of the building

## Schedule and game access
- Teams are told to review the tournament schedule on the association website: https://dwgha.com/
- The team schedule is specifically available here: https://dwgha.com/Tournaments/2006/Teams/467/
- Game codes are generated in the Ramp portal once the schedule is loaded, approximately one week before the tournament
- Teams are instructed to download the Ramp Gamesheets App on a phone or device to access electronic game sheets, rosters, and player management
- The app is free and available through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store
- Game results, scores, standings, and live updates are posted on the DWGHA website

## Important operational information
- Teams are asked to share vendor information with their players/parents
- Vendors mentioned include:
  - Les Serota (located near Pad 3-4), selling novelty items and providing samples to teams
- Additional service mentioned:
  - Touch with Care Hair Braiding Service, with booking information available on the website
